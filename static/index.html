REPRODUCIBLE EXPLORATION

Assembly research lab

Five local ARM64 kernels measure signal moments, RSSI distributions, sliding-window sums, dot products and payload bit changes. Rust validates inputs and interprets the measurements. No radio permissions or additional probes are used.

Analyze one port on one target. Historical network contexts are available. Successful recorded TCP timings only; samples are quantized to 0.1 ms, and values outside 0–3276.7 ms are excluded.

Up to 10,000 packets and 64 addresses. RSSI is not a calibrated distance measurement. Packet-order correlation is not fixed-time correlation.

Choose a source. No synthetic observations are inserted into your database.

Kernel microbenchmark

Compare the assembly kernels with optimized Rust references on this phone. Five warmed batches, 100 calls per batch. This measures the kernels, not whole-app performance. Ratios below 1 mean the assembly was slower.

BRING YOUR OWN OBSERVER

Sensor gateway

Receive BLE scan logs or IP service observations from a companion. The server stays on localhost; a remote sensor needs an authenticated tunnel to this phone.

Ingestion format

POST JSON to /api/v2/ingest with Authorization: Bearer TOKEN. BLE payload: {"source":"sensor.log","text":"TIME ADDRESS RSSI HEXDATA"}. IP payload: 

{"observations":[{"host":"192.168.1.20","status":"responding","services":[{"port":80,"state":"open"}]}]}

Sensor reports retain separate provenance and never overwrite local scan results. Revoking a credential stops new submissions.
